My Work

I am a socio-cognitive researcher specialising in how people apply critical thinking skills to understand information they encounter online, particularly conspiracy theories. I approach my research using a range of methods, such as designing novel psychometric tools, large-scale quantitative experiments, qualitative interviews, public participant involvement (PPI) projects, and developing technological tools to teach educational outcomes such as interactive video games.
